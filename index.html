A spark that became a legacy

Rooted in family.
Made for wonder.

It began with a borrowed trailer, a hand-typed permit, and a county fair that needed an unforgettable finale. In 1979, Ray and Ellen Kovacs fired the first Classic Fireworks show in Hartsville, Pennsylvania.

Four decades and more than 1,200 displays later, that same feeling still guides us. Today, a second generation of our family works alongside licensed pyrotechnicians and show designers to create something extraordinary, every time.

The technology has changed. The promise hasn’t: thoughtful design, meticulous preparation, and a sky worth remembering.
